# GRAMMAR UNIT 1: PRESENT TENSE REVISION

## 1. Read the grammar notes and complete the three tense names.
1. We use the **present simple** to:
   * Give factual information.
   * Talk about routine activities.
   * Use many state verbs.
2. We use the **present continuous** to:
   * Describe an action happening now.
   * Describe a temporary situation.
   * Talk about future arrangements.
   * Talk about a changing situation.
3. We use the **present perfect** to talk about:
   * Past actions that continue to the present.
   * Personal experiences with ever and never.
   * Past actions connected to the present.

## 3. Write a question for each answer.
* **a. Mike prints all his documents in the office.**
  * **Question:** Where does Mike print all his documents?
* **b. The team is meeting next Tuesday.**
  * **Question:** When is the team meeting?
* **c. We're looking for a new PC because this one is old-fashioned.**
  * **Question:** Why are you looking for a new PC?
* **d. Brad maintains the computers in our company.**
  * **Question:** Who maintains the computers in your company?

## 5. Write complete sentences from the prompts.
* a. Pete / work / here / 2008
  * **Sentence:** Pete has worked here since 2008.
* b. She / have / this laptop / more than three years
  * **Sentence:** She has had this laptop for more than three years.
* c. The company / not post / a message on its Twitter profile / the past two weeks
  * **Sentence:** The company hasn't posted a message on its Twitter profile for the past two weeks.
* d. We / not update / the antivirus software / last year
  * **Sentence:** We haven't updated the antivirus software since last year.
* e. I / not upgrade / my workstation / I bought it
  * **Sentence:** I haven't upgraded my workstation since I bought it.
